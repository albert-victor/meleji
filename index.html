A few useful things

Before You Arrive.

How do I book a stay?

Send your dates, guest numbers and room preference through our booking enquiry. The team will confirm availability, rates and next steps.

Can I combine my stay with a safari?

Yes. Tell us the places you hope to visit and how much time you have. The team can discuss a route alongside your accommodation enquiry.

Can I add meals and local experiences?

Mention food preferences, dietary needs and activities in your enquiry so the hosts can discuss suitable options before you arrive.
